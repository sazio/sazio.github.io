    About

    Vision, from photoreceptors to priors.

    I am a computational neuroscientist working between machine learning and vision neuroscience, currently a Guest Research Associate in Yukiyasu Kamitani's lab at Kyoto University.

    I did my PhD at the Vision Institute (Sorbonne) and École Normale Supérieure with Ulisse Ferrari, Peter Neri, Olivier Marre and Matthew Chalk.

    Most of my work starts from large-scale neural recordings and asks what principles a good visual code should follow, then turns those principles into models that also work as machine learning architectures.
